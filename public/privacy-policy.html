Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

This policy explains what information Nexyronix Solutions Private Limited ("Nexyronix", "we", "us") collects through nexyronix.com, why we collect it, and how it's handled. It only describes what this website actually does — nothing here is aspirational.

1. Who we are

Nexyronix Solutions Private Limited, a software and digital solutions company registered in India, operating from 80, 4th Floor, Mango Meadows Layout, North Main Gate, Bidarahalli, Bengaluru 560049. You can reach us at info@nexyronix.com or +91 94820 81830.

2. Information we collect

The only place this website collects personal information is the contact form. Depending on what you fill in, that may include:

  • Your name and email address (required)
  • Phone number and organization/company name (optional)
  • The type of enquiry you selected, and any budget range or "how did you hear about us" answer you choose to share (optional)
  • The message you write
  • For Internship or Career Opportunity enquiries only: an optional resume file (PDF, DOC or DOCX)

We don't collect anything beyond what you actively type or upload into that form.

3. How we use this information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to read, evaluate and respond to your enquiry — including internship or career interest — and for our own reasonable internal record-keeping about that communication. We do not sell, rent, or use this information for advertising.

4. Resume uploads

If you upload a resume, it is attached directly to the internal email notification our team receives and is not saved to any database or file storage by this website. There is no persistent copy of your resume held by the website itself — once the notification email is sent, the file exists only in that email (and wherever our team's own email provider stores it, which is outside this website's control).

5. Email delivery

Enquiry notifications are sent using Resend, a third-party transactional email service. Resend processes the contents of your submission solely to deliver that one email to us — it acts as our service provider for that purpose.

6. Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting infrastructure may generate standard technical logs (such as IP address, request timestamps and basic request metadata) for operational, security and troubleshooting purposes. These are not used to build any profile of you.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we do not currently use Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics or advertising tool. This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts; retrieving those font files involves your browser connecting to Google's servers, which may receive your IP address as a normal part of that request.

8. Third-party services we actually use

  • Resend — delivers contact-form notifications to our team.
  • Google Fonts — hosts the web fonts used on this site.
  • WhatsApp — the WhatsApp button on this site is a link to an external chat; if you use it, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices, not ours.

We do not use any other third-party service to process information submitted through this website.

9. Data retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to it and for legitimate business record-keeping, and not longer than needed for that purpose.

10. Data security

Submissions are sent over HTTPS and validated on our server before being processed. We take reasonable technical measures to protect information you submit, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights

You can contact us at info@nexyronix.com to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion. We aim to handle personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

12. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
