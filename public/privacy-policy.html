Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information Nexyronix Solutions Private Limited ("Nexyronix", "we", "us") collects through nexyronix.com, why we collect it, and how it's handled. It only describes what this website actually does — nothing here is aspirational.

1. Who we are

Nexyronix Solutions Private Limited, a software and digital solutions company registered in India, operating from 80, 4th Floor, Mango Meadows Layout, North Main Gate, Bidarahalli, Bengaluru 560049. You can reach us at info@nexyronix.com or +91 94820 81830.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. The contact form is the only place where you give us personal information. Depending on what you fill in, that may include:

  • Your name and email address (required)
  • Phone number and organization/company name (optional)
  • The type of enquiry you selected, and any budget range or "how did you hear about us" answer you choose to share (optional)
  • The message you write
  • For Internship or Career Opportunity enquiries only: an optional resume file (PDF, DOC or DOCX)

Information recorded automatically. Like any website, ours receives technical information with every visit — such as your IP address and browser details — and some of it may be recorded in server logs. Section 6 explains what is recorded, why, and how long it is kept.

3. How we use this information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to read, evaluate and respond to your enquiry — including internship or career interest — and for our own reasonable internal record-keeping about that communication. We do not sell, rent, or use this information for advertising.

4. Resume uploads

If you upload a resume, it is attached directly to the internal email notification our team receives and is not saved to any database or file storage by this website. There is no persistent copy of your resume held by the website itself. Once the notification email is sent, the file travels with that email through our email delivery service and is kept in our business mailbox, as described in section 5.

5. Email delivery and our mailbox

When you submit the form, our server sends its contents — including any resume — to our team as an email, using Resend, a US-based transactional email service. We use Resend only to deliver that email to us. Resend may keep records of the emails it delivers under its own privacy policy and terms. Your email address is set as the reply-to address so that we can answer you.

The email is delivered to our business mailbox, which receives mail through GoDaddy's email service. We read and respond to enquiries from our business email, where the enquiry and any resume are kept.

6. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on Microsoft Azure (Azure App Service) in Microsoft's Central India region. Every request to the site, including contact-form submissions, passes through Microsoft's infrastructure; Microsoft acts as our service provider for hosting.

To deliver the site and keep it secure and reliable, the following is processed and may be logged:

  • Technical request information — your IP address, the date and time, the address of the page or file requested (including any query string in it, such as the campaign tags in a link like ?utm_source=…), the response status, your browser's user-agent and the referring page. Microsoft's hosting platform processes this for every request and may record it in request logs.
  • Performance and error information — for example response times and failed requests, as measured by the hosting platform, and the error messages our own server writes, such as when an enquiry notification can't be sent.

Request logs don't include what you write in the contact form or any resume you upload: the form is sent in the body of the request, which is not logged, and our site never puts personal details into page addresses. If personal details are typed into a page address directly (for example in a query string), they would be recorded like any other address. Error logs are not designed to hold form contents, but an error message can occasionally quote part of a request, or of a reply from Resend, that could not be processed. Error messages written by our own server software pass through automated redaction that removes email addresses; this reduces the risk of an address being logged but may not catch every possible format, and it does not remove other details or apply to the request logs described above. We keep these records only for as long as we need them for operation, security and troubleshooting, and use them only for those purposes — never to build a profile of you, and never for analytics or advertising.

7. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising, and we do not currently use Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics or advertising tool. The logging described in section 6 happens only on the server side: it places no cookies and adds no scripts to your browser. This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts; retrieving those font files involves your browser connecting to Google's servers, which may receive your IP address as a normal part of that request.

8. Third-party services we actually use

  • Microsoft Azure — hosts this website and the technical logs described in section 6.
  • Resend — delivers contact-form notifications, including any resume, to our team (section 5).
  • GoDaddy — provides the email service through which enquiries reach our business mailbox (section 5).
  • Google Fonts — hosts the web fonts used on this site.
  • WhatsApp — the WhatsApp button on this site is a link to an external chat; if you use it, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices, not ours.

We do not use any other third-party service to process information submitted through this website.

9. Where your information is processed

The website is hosted in India (Microsoft Azure, Central India region). Resend, a US-based company, delivers our notification emails from outside India, so the contents of an enquiry, including any resume, are processed outside India while the notification is delivered. GoDaddy's email service and Google's font servers may also process or store information outside India.

10. Data retention

  • Enquiries and resumes in our mailbox: for as long as reasonably necessary to respond and for legitimate business record-keeping, and not longer. You can ask us to delete them at any time (section 12).
  • Resend's records: any records Resend keeps of the emails it delivers are governed by its own privacy policy and terms.
  • Technical logs (section 6): only for as long as needed for operation, security and troubleshooting.

11. Data security

Submissions are sent over HTTPS and validated on our server before being processed. The key our server uses to send email is kept in Microsoft Azure Key Vault, not in the website's code. We take reasonable technical measures to protect information you submit, but no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Your rights

You can contact us at info@nexyronix.com to ask what information we hold about you, request a correction, or request deletion. We aim to handle personal data in accordance with applicable data protection law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

13. Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
